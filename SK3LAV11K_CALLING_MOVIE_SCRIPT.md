# SK3LAV11K CALLING — THE MOVIE SCRIPT
## Revised edition (producer critique applied) · 31 Oct 2026

The whole COSMICROW campaign told as one film, in the order a player sees it. COSMICROW is a rowing game: the player rows on a Concept2 rowing machine, and the stroke flies the ship.

- **PLAYABLE** marks a chapter the player rows or plays. Radio lines in a playable chapter are triggered by distance (shown in metres) or by game events, and they queue so they never overlap.
- Everything else is a **CUTSCENE**.
- **PULL GATE** marks a cutscene moment that waits for the player's strokes on the rower (or taps), at any speed.

**The rule of this story:** every character leaves something that helps someone else survive. The final victory contains the work of everyone who did not reach it.

**Fleet at the start:** the Aurora and seven wingmen (Valkyrie, Raven, Nebula, Tempest, Titan, Eclipse, Phoenix), plus TJ Pacer from the Rings on.

**Chapters (unchanged):** 1 DO11AD011 (laser run) · 2 Formation (750 m row) · 3 The Rings (laser run) · 4 Descent (500 m row) · 5 SK3LAV11K (600 m row) · 6 Iron Rain (tug of war) · 7 Broken Formation (laser run) · 8 SK3LHOVD1 (tug of war) · 9 The Hunt (600 m row) · 10 Neon City (500 m row).

**Technology rules**
- Warden-9 can hear the old beacons and attack where they burn. It cannot command them.
- The fleet's ships carry network receivers for navigation. Kept isolated, they are safe. Opened, they let Warden-9 in.
- DO11AD011's assembly floors were on the network. The old foundry beneath them never was.
- Warden-9's shield rings grow back if the rower slows down.

### Prologue (cutscene)

**FADE IN. BLACK.** *Music: lonely.* Text crawls up through the stars:

> SK3LAV11K CALLING
>
> In the year 3026 the network held everything. Every ship, every city, every light.

>

>

>

>

>

>
